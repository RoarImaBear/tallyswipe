TallySwipe

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

TallySwipe collects no data. Everything you count stays on your iPhone.

TallySwipe is made by an independent developer. This policy covers the TallySwipe app for iPhone and this website.

What stays on your iPhone

Your counts, samples, saved counts and sets, and your counters' names and colours are stored only on your iPhone. The developer can't see them. Your iPhone includes them in its backups, to iCloud or a computer, if you back up.

The app also keeps a small record of which days you've counted on, in your iPhone's keychain, so reinstalling the app doesn't restart the free days. It holds those dates and nothing about what you counted.

Purchases

The unlock is sold through the App Store, so Apple handles the purchase. The developer never receives your name, Apple Account or payment details; Apple reports sales to developers without them. Apple's privacy policy covers the purchase itself.

What TallySwipe doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • The app itself sends nothing over the internet.

If you've chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Sharing your counts

When you copy or export counts, they go only where you choose to send them.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

TallySwipe collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated before the app is, with a new date at the top.
