TallySwipe

A tally counter you can use without looking.

Getting started

  • Swipe toward a number to add one. Every count clicks, so you can keep your eyes on what you're counting.
  • Hold a number to save it, reset it, step it with − and +, or give it a name and colour.
  • Hold the centre to switch between four counters and one counter with samples, or to save or reset everything.
  • In single mode, swipe down further to take a sample. The count goes into the list and starts again from zero.
  • Pull up from the bottom for Saved, where you can group counts into sets, view them as a table, and copy or export them as CSV or JSON.

The ? button in the app shows all of this at any time.

Free days and the unlock

Everything in TallySwipe is free for your first 3 counting days. A counting day is any day you add a count, and the day you start is the first.

After that, adding new counts needs a one-time purchase. There's no subscription and there are no ads.

Whatever you've already counted stays yours. Without the unlock, you can still view, save, copy, export, reset and delete it.

Restoring a purchase

If you bought the unlock before or on another iPhone, open the help (?) and tap Restore Purchase. Family Sharing is supported, so people in your family group get the unlock too.

Your data

Your counts stay on your iPhone. TallySwipe has no accounts, and the app sends nothing anywhere; purchases go through Apple. Your counts are included in your iPhone's backups.

Deleting the app deletes your counts, so export anything you want to keep first. The privacy policy has the details.
